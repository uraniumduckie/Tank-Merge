➤ Merging & Destruction

Tanks merge or explode when they touch — either by dragging them together or letting them collide on their own.

Same nation, same class, same tier: Merge into the next tier.

Same nation, different tier/class: Bump apart.

Different nations: The higher tier destroys the lower one. Same tier, different class — the TD wins. Same tier, same class — both explode.

Each nation has unique tank and tank destroyer (TD) lines. Earn new tanks by merging them on the field.

➤ Taming tanks

Click the main field to spawn a random Tier 1 tank. Drag a tank onto the 📦 box to send it to its nation's field.

Tanks on nation fields don't interact with each other. Use the ☰ button to view unlocked tanks. Tanks on nation fields are your pets. Love them.

➤ Sending to Battle

On a nation field, select a tank and press Enter (or click the ⚔ button) to send it to battle. You can chat with other players or kill them. Your choice.
